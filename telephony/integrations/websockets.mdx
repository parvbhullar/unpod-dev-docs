**WebSockets** - raw audio streaming for any bot you run yourself.

Reply to a call with `<Stream>`, or start a stream on a live call over REST, and Unpod
opens a WebSocket to your server. It sends the caller's audio in 20 ms frames, plays back
the audio your bot returns, and signs every connection with your webhook secret. Your bot
can be written in any language; with Pipecat, `UnpodFrameSerializer` does the protocol for
you.

<Note>
**Preview.** Audio streaming is switched on per org. Ask the Unpod team to enable it.
</Note>
