The **no-code** path. Configure Twilio as a SIP provider in the Unpod Studio, attach
it to a number, and route global programmable voice through your agent. Prefer code?
See the [API guide](/telephony/integrations/twilio/api).

<Note>
**You need:** an Unpod number on a Bridge, and a Twilio account with an **Account SID**
and **Auth Token** (Twilio Console → Account → API keys & tokens).
</Note>

## Configure the number with Twilio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Configure on your number">
    The number is already on your Bridge. Click **Configure** to activate SIP trunking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a SIP provider">
    You are redirected to the provider picker. Click **Configure** in front of **Twilio**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Twilio credentials">
    Fill the mandatory fields:
    - **Name** - any label for this configuration.
    - **Account SID** - your Twilio account identifier (starts with `AC…`).
    - **Auth Token** - the secret paired with the SID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy credentials from Twilio">
    In the **Twilio Console** dashboard, copy the **Account SID** and **Auth Token**
    from **Account Info**, and paste them into the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and Configure">
    Click **Verify and Configure**. The configuration appears under **Active Providers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the provider to your number">
    Click **Select** next to your Twilio configuration under **Active Providers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set channels and agent">
    Open **Settings** and submit:
    - **Concurrency Channels** - simultaneous calls allowed.
    - **Enter Agent** - the agent that answers the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish** at the top of the dashboard. The number is ready for inbound and outbound calls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Twilio also supports **Elastic SIP Trunking** - point a Twilio trunk at your Unpod
origin endpoint for a pure-SIP setup. See [Create a trunk](/telephony/trunks/create-trunk).
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Verify fails | Wrong SID/token | Re-copy from the Twilio Console Account Info |
| Calls rejected | Geo-permissions off | Enable the destination region in Twilio Voice settings |
| Agent silent | No agent bound | Set **Enter Agent** in trunk Settings |
