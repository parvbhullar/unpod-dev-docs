Train an agent on your use case. Use it like a model - over chat, realtime
voice, or a phone number.

A playbook + a small model trained on your workflows - or any third-party
model - served over whichever surface fits your stack. Your orchestration,
speech, and transport stay exactly where they are.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Personalised" icon="target">
    A model trained on your data and workflows - not a 100-page prompt on a
    general model.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fast & cheap" icon="zap">
    ~1B-parameter models built for realtime. Cheaper to run, faster to answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your stack stays" icon="plug">
    LiveKit, Pipecat, Vapi, chat - or our phone number. No re-platform.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Use it like a model

| Surface | I/O | Live in |
|---|---|---|
| [Chat](/get-started/chat) | text in, text out | 1 URL swap |
| [Realtime](/get-started/realtime) | audio in, audio out | 1 WebSocket |
| [Phone](/get-started/phone) | a phone number | 1 click |

Same trained agent, three ways in. Pick by what you already run.
