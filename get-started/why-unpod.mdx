Unpod runs the intelligence of a real-time agent: a playbook + a small model
trained on your use case - or any third-party model - with the speech and
telephony infra to run it. Adoption is one endpoint change.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Personalised" icon="target">
    A model trained on your data and workflows - not a 100-page prompt on a
    general model.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fast & cheap" icon="zap">
    ~1B-parameter models built for realtime. Cheaper to run, faster to answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your stack stays" icon="plug">
    LiveKit, Pipecat, Vapi, chat - or our phone number. No re-platform.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Deploy at any depth

| Mode | I/O | Live in |
|---|---|---|
| [SLM inference](/get-started/slm-inference) - the brain | text in, text out | 1 URL swap |
| [STS inference](/get-started/sts-inference) - the voice | audio in, audio out | 1 WebSocket |
| [Voice Agent](/get-started/voice-agent) - the agent | a phone number | 1 click |

Each mode adds one layer. Pick by what you already run.
