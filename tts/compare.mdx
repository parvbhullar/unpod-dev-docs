## How soon each one starts speaking

Time to first audio is the wait between sending text and hearing sound. On a call, it is the pause
before the agent replies.

<Tts view="latency" data={TTS_DATA} />

<Accordion title="How we compared">
  - **Lines.** Four calls per language, from industries voice agents work in: a hospital
    appointment, a delivery address, a train ticket and a policy renewal. They were written for this
    comparison. Numbers are sent as digits, the way an LLM sends them, so each engine's own number
    reading is part of the test.
  - **Voices.** Each provider's own native voice for the language where it has one: a female voice
    for the hospital and policy calls, a male voice for the delivery and train calls. When a provider has no voice of
    that gender in the language, the card shows the voice that was used instead. Unpod uses Kanika
    and Bunty in every language.
  - **One take each, default settings.** No retries and no cherry-picking. Recorded {TTS_DATA.recorded}.
  - **First audio** is the median time from sending the request to the first audio byte, over all 20
    lines, on each provider's streaming HTTP API. Every request ran from the same machine in India,
    one at a time, over a warm connection.
</Accordion>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are these the real prices?">
    Yes. They are the pay-as-you-go list prices on each provider's own pricing page on
    {TTS_DATA.recorded}, with no volume discounts. Unpod's price is not listed on this page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why per character and not per minute?">
    Every provider here bills by the characters you send, not by the length of the audio. To show a
    price per minute or per hour, a minute of speech is counted as 1,000 characters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the first-audio times measured the same way?">
    Yes. We measured every other provider ourselves on {TTS_DATA.recorded}: the median time from request to
    first audio byte over the same 20 lines, on each provider's streaming HTTP API, from one machine
    in India, one request at a time over a warm connection. Unpod is not timed here: its clips were
    recorded on a test copy of our server, not the production API, so its time would not be comparable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How were the recordings made?">
    One take per line with each provider's default settings, no retries and no editing. Numbers were
    sent as digits, the way an LLM writes them, so each engine reads them its own way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a card show a different voice on some lines?">
    Each provider uses its own native voice for the language: a female voice for the hospital and
    policy calls and a male voice for the delivery and train calls. When a provider has no voice of that gender in the
    language, the card shows the voice that was used instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
