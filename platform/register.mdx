## How to Get Access to the Unpod.ai Dashboard?

### Step 1
Open [https://unpod.ai/auth/signup/](https://unpod.ai/auth/signup/). The **Create an account** page opens.

### Step 2
Click **Sign up with Google** to use your Google account, or fill in the form:
- **Full Name**
- **Phone Number** - pick your country code, then enter the number.
- **Email Address**
- **Password** - the two rules under the field, **8 characters, 1 number & 1 special character** and **1 lowercase & uppercase letter**, turn green once your password meets them. The form does not submit until both are met.

Keep **I accept the terms and privacy policy** checked (it is checked by default) and click **Sign Up**.

If you already have an account, click **Sign In** at the bottom of the form.

### Step 3
After an email sign-up, the **Verify Your OTP** screen opens with the message "We've sent a 6-digit verification code to your email address." Enter the 6-digit code from your email and click **Verify**.

- **Resend** becomes active when the "Resend OTP in" timer (2 minutes) runs out.
- If you mistyped your email, click the edit icon next to it, enter the correct address, and click **Update**.

Google sign-up skips this step.
