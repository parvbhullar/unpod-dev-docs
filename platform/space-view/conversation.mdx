## Agent

<Note>
In the Space sidebar this section is now named **Agent**. It was earlier named Conversation.
</Note>

Use this section to talk to the space's agent.

When no conversation is open, the page shows the agent's animated sphere and a message box with the placeholder **Ask me anything...**.

1. Type your message. To attach files, click the paperclip button (up to 10 files).
2. Press **Enter** to send. Press **Shift + Enter** for a new line.
3. A new conversation opens with your message.

Your conversations are listed in the panel next to the left sidebar. Click one to open it again.
