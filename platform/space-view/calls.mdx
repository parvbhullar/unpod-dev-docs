## Calls

Call View is a dedicated interface where you can manage voice communication activities - including live calls, call history, transcripts, summaries, and call related actions - all within the context of that space. This section provides a centralized space to initiate voice calls directly from within the space, view ongoing voice sessions, and manage call settings.

<img src="/images/Space Mode3.png" alt="Call View Screenshot" />

### Make a call

1. Open **Calls**. When no call is selected, click the **New Call** card.
2. In the **Start Calls for Contacts** drawer, select the contacts you want to call and click **Call Now**.

<img src="/images/Space Mode4.png" alt="Call Selection Screenshot" />

3. A call dialog opens. Its title reads **Call to** followed by the contact's name, or by the number of contacts when you selected more than one.
4. If needed, edit the instructions for the call, and pick the agent in the agent selector at the bottom left.
5. By default the call is placed right away. To change when to call, click **Schedule** and choose:
   - **Now** - Calls right away.
   - **Auto Schedule** - Calls within the agent's pre-configured calling hours.
   - **Custom Schedule** - Calls on a date and time you pick.

   Click **Cancel** (the same button) to hide the schedule options again.
6. Click **Start Call**.

If your workspace has no phone number yet, the dialog shows **Please add a phone number in Telephony.** with an **Open Telephony** link, and **Start Call** is disabled.

### Call details

Select a call in the left sidebar to open it. The header shows the call's direction (inbound or outbound) and status, such as Pending, In Progress or Completed.

<img src="/images/Space Mode6.png" alt="Call Status Screenshot" />

<Note>
Call times in the Calls section are shown in IST (UTC+5:30), whatever timezone your browser uses.
</Note>

A call has three tabs - Overview, Conversation and Activity.

### Overview

The Overview tab shows these cards:

- **Summary** - What happened on the call, with metrics such as call score, sentiment, engagement, duration and tone, the **Next Step**, and whether a **Follow Up** is needed
- **Call Details** - When the call was dialed, connected and ended, who ended it, the user and assistant numbers, and who triggered the call
- **Attempts** - Each attempt made to reach the contact
- **Structured Data** - Data the agent extracted from the call
- **Evaluation** - A quality evaluation of the call. If none exists yet, click **Generate evaluation** on the card to create one. Evaluation is available only for calls longer than 1 minute.

A card stays empty until its data is collected and processed.

<img src="/images/Space Mode7.jpeg" alt="Overview Tab Screenshot" />

### Conversation

The Conversation tab shows the call recording and the transcript of the chat between the customer and the agent, so you can read the key points without listening to the whole recording.

<img src="/images/Space Mode7.jpeg" alt="Conversation Tab Screenshot" />

### Activity

The Activity tab shows the **Call Flow** and the **Call Output** of the call.
