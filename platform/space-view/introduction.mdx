## Introduction to Space Mode

To open the Space View, click **Space View** at the bottom of the left sidebar. In the Space View the same button reads **Studio View** and takes you back. Pick the space you want to work in from the space switcher at the top of the left sidebar.

{/* TODO(UNPO-403): new screenshot — Space View of a contact space: left sidebar with the space switcher, Inbox, Calls, People, Analytics and Agent, and the Studio View button at the bottom */}

The left sidebar of the Space View has these sections:

- **Inbox** - the agent's conversation queue, with All, Unread, WhatsApp and Email views
- **Calls** - the space's calls
- **People** - the space's contacts. This entry is named after the space's content type: **People** in a contact space, **Inbox** in an email space, **Documents** in a document space, and **Knowledge** otherwise.
- **Analytics** - activity metrics for the space
- **Agent** - talk to the space's agent. This section was earlier named Conversation.

At the top right of a space page, after the share button, click the three-dot menu (⋮). It has three options:

- **Edit Space** - opens the Edit Space drawer
- **Space Token** - copies the space token to your clipboard
- **Call Logs** - opens the **Download Call Logs** drawer. See [Call Logs](/platform/studio-view/call-logs).

The menu is shown only to users who can edit the space.

## Edit Space

Click **Edit Space** in the three-dot menu. In a contact space the drawer has three tabs:

- **Basic Info** - the space name and description
- **Table Schema** - the fields each contact has
- **Link Agent** - the agent linked to the space

In other spaces the drawer shows only the basic information.

<img src="/images/Space Mode1b.png" alt="Edit Space Screenshot" />

The main part to edit is the table schema. The default schema may have fields your business does not need, or miss fields it does need. Click the **Table Schema** tab to see the current fields.

<img src="/images/Space Mode1c.png" alt="Table Schema Screenshot" />

Name and Contact Number cannot be deleted from the schema. Every other field has a delete button. Each field also has a **Required** checkbox: when it is ticked, the field must be filled in when you add a contact. To add a field, click **Add Field**.

When you click **Add Field**, pick the type of field to add, such as Text, Text area, File, Number, Email address, Phone number, URL, Date, Time, Date and Time, Check List, Single select or Multi select. Click **Save** at the bottom of the drawer to save the schema.

## Space Token

Space Token is a unique access credential used to identify and authorize a user within a specific Space.

It is needed to secure access, control permissions, track activity, and ensure that actions (calls, docs, analytics) are correctly linked to the right Space and users.
