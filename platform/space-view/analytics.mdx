## Analytics

The Analytics section shows call metrics for the space. Click the refresh button at the top right to reload the data.

At the top, filter the data by **Call Type** (All Calls, Inbound or Outbound), by agent (**Select Agent**) and by **Date**, then click **Apply**. Click **Reset** to clear the filters. On a phone, the filters are behind a **Filters** button.

The page has these parts:

- **Where all N calls sit right now** - summary tiles (Numbers used, Minutes Consumed, Voicemails, Total cost, Average cost, Avg Minutes Consumed) and the calls by status: Scheduled, Connected, Not Connected and Failed
- **Structured Data** - goal metrics for an output you pick: Structured Data, Sentiment or End Call Reason
- **Call Activity Peak Times** - a chart of total and connected calls over the day, with the busiest window
- A table of calls with two tabs, **By Agent** and **By Number**, showing Total, Connected, Not Connected and Failed calls, Billable (Min) and Cost
