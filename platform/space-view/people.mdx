## People

With the help of this section, you will be able to add contacts to the space of your organization. When no contact is selected, the main panel shows a **New Contact** card. Click it. You can also open the three-dot menu (⋮) next to the search box above the contact list and click **Add Contact**.

<img src="/images/Space Mode9.png" alt="New Contact Screenshot" />

The main panel on the right changes to a form with two tabs, **Import** and **Add Manually**. You stay on the same page; the URL does not change. The fields you can fill in come from the space's table schema (see [Edit Space](/platform/space-view/introduction#edit-space)).

<img src="/images/Space Mode10.png" alt="Contact Options Screenshot" />

**Import** is the tab that opens first. Drag your files onto the upload area, or click it to choose them. You can upload up to 5 files in CSV, TSV or XLSX format. To get a file with the right columns, click **Download Schema** and choose **Download CSV** or **Download XLSX**. Then click **Save**.

**Add Manually** shows one input for each field in the table schema. Required fields must be filled in. Fill in the fields and click **Save**. Click **Cancel** on either tab to go back without saving.

<img src="/images/Space Mode11.png" alt="Add Manually Screenshot" />
