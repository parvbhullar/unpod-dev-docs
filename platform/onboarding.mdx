After you sign up and verify your email (see [Register](/platform/register)), Unpod walks you through a short setup. The steps below are for a new account that does not belong to an organization yet.

### Step 1: Pick your role
The **Which role describes you the best?** screen shows four cards: **Developer**, **Founder**, **Product Manager**, and **Other**. Click the one that fits you.

### Step 2: Choose what to start with
On **What would you like to get started with?**, click one of **Agent Studio**, **AI Agents**, **Analytics**, **Contact Center**, **Context Engine**, **Speech Infra**, or **Telephony**. Every option takes you to the business identity step. **Back** returns you to the role screen.

### Step 3: Set up your business identity
The **Set up your business identity** screen (marked **Step 1 · Your business**) creates your organization. You only see it if you do not have an organization yet.

- **Business name & Website URL** - type your business name, or your website (for example `https://acme.com`). When you enter a website, Unpod waits about 2 seconds, shows **Fetching your business details…**, and fills in the business name and domain from the site. Public email domains such as gmail.com are rejected; use your company domain. If the fetch fails, you see "Could not fetch your website details. Please continue manually." and can fill in the details yourself.
- **Domain** - your company domain, for example `yourcompany.com`. It is filled in for you when you enter a website.

Click **Continue**. If the website scan also produced a ready agent, you skip ahead to [Step 6](#step-6-review-the-ai-identity) with the fields prefilled. Otherwise you go to Step 4.

### Step 4: Describe your agent
The **Describe the AI calling agent you want to create** screen (marked **Step 1 of 4 · Tell us what you want**) starts the agent setup. A progress bar at the top shows the four steps: **Start**, **AI Identity**, **Voice Profile**, **Launch**.

Pick an input mode:
- **Describe in chat** (selected by default) - in **What should your agent do?**, describe your business, who the agent talks to, and the kind of calls it handles. The hint under the box suggests covering your business type, customer type, agent task, language, and handoff rules. Under **or start from a template**, you can instead click **Choose** on a ready-made agent template.
- **Fetch from URL** - paste your site into **Paste your website URL**. Unpod scans the site and turns what it finds into an agent.

Click **Generate Agent**. Choosing a template skips generation and prefills the next steps from the template.

### Step 5: Wait while the agent is built
The **Building your AI Profile** screen shows while Unpod generates the agent. It usually takes 15–30 seconds. If generation fails, you return to Step 4 with an error message.

### Step 6: Review the AI Identity
The **Review the AI Identity** screen (marked **Step 2 of 4 · Generated for you**) shows what was generated. Edit any field:
- **Name** (required)
- **Greeting** (required) - the first line your agent says when a session starts.
- **Behavior script** - the instructions the agent runs on.

Click **Continue**. This creates the agent.

### Step 7: Pick a voice profile
The **Pick a tone** screen (marked **Step 3 of 4**) lists the available voice profiles. Each card has a play button to preview the voice, plus the profile's gender, provider, languages, **Quality**, **Latency**, and **Cost**. Click a card to select it, then click **Continue**. **Continue** stays disabled until you select a profile. **Back** returns you to the AI Identity step.

### Step 8: Launch
The **Ready to launch** screen (marked **Step 4 of 4 · Almost there**) summarizes your agent:
- **Identity** - the agent's name and description.
- **Behavior** - the length of the behavior script.
- **Tone & Reach** - the voice profile, gender, and language.

Below the summary, **After you launch** lists the next things you can set up. Click **Launch profile**.

### Step 9: Refine the agent in AI Studio
After **Launch profile**, you land on your agent's page in **AI Studio**. From there you can refine the agent and open the rest of the dashboard from the left sidebar.

<Note>
To create another agent later, click **+** in the AI Studio agent list. It opens the same flow at Step 4, and the business identity step is skipped because you already have an organization.
</Note>

Now you have complete access to the [dashboard](https://unpod.ai).
