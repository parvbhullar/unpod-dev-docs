Voice AI agents are replacing traditional IVR systems and transforming how businesses handle calls. Unpod makes it possible to build a production-ready voice agent in under 30 minutes - no ML background required. This guide walks through every step.

## What We're Building

A customer support agent that:
- Answers inbound calls for a SaaS product
- Has a defined persona and voice
- Can look up information from uploaded docs
- Hands off to a human when needed

---

## Step 1: Create Your Agent in AI Studio

Log in to [unpod.ai](https://unpod.ai) and open **AI Studio** from the left sidebar. In the **Agents** list, click **+**. This opens the agent setup flow:

1. **Start** - describe what the agent should do in **What should your agent do?**, pick a template under "or start from a template", or switch to **Fetch from URL** and paste your website. Click **Generate Agent**.
2. **AI Identity** - review the generated **Name** (e.g. Aria), **Greeting** and **Behavior script**, then click **Continue**. This creates the agent.
3. **Voice Profile** - on the "Pick a tone" screen, pick a voice profile and click **Continue**.
4. **Launch** - review the summary and click **Launch profile**.

You land on the agent's page in AI Studio, with the tabs **Persona**, **Voice Profile**, **Advanced**, **Analysis**, **Integration** and **Identity**. See [Create Your AI Identity](/platform/onboarding) for the full flow.

The agent's **handle** is how you reference it in the API and telephony config. To copy it, open the **⋮** menu at the top right of the agent page and click **Copy Handle**.

---

## Step 2: Define the Persona

The persona determines how your agent thinks, speaks, and behaves. Open the **Persona** tab.

### System Prompt

Under **CONVERSATION MODE**, open **Prompt** and write a clear system prompt that defines:
- Role and purpose
- Tone and communication style
- What the agent should and should not do

You can also start from a template: switch to the **Template** tab, click **Select template** and pick one; its prompt is copied into the editor.

Example:

```
You are Aria, a friendly and professional customer support agent for Acme SaaS. 
Your goal is to help users resolve issues with their account, billing, and product features.

Rules:
- Always greet the caller by name if available
- Be concise - callers are on the phone, not reading
- If you cannot resolve an issue, offer to connect them with a human agent
- Never make promises about refunds or SLA violations without checking policy
```

### Greeting Message

Under **GREETING**, open **Greeting message** and enter the first thing your agent says when the call connects:

```
Hi, you've reached Acme support. I'm Aria, your AI assistant. 
How can I help you today?
```

Click **Save** at the bottom right of the tab. See [Persona](/platform/studio-view/persona) for every section.

---

## Step 3: Configure the Voice Profile

Open the **Voice Profile** tab. To reuse a saved setup, open the **Voice Profile** section and click **Manage Profiles**. To set the voice by hand, open the **Voice** section and choose:

- **Voice Provider** and **Voice model**
- **Synthesized voice**: the voice your agent speaks with
- **Voice Speed**: 0.9-1.0 works well for support calls
- **Voice Temperature**: lower values sound more consistent, higher values more expressive

The **Transcriber** section sets the speech-to-text provider and the **Language for transcription**. Click **Save** when you're done.

---

## Step 4: Choose Your AI Model

On the same **Voice Profile** tab, open the **LLM** section and select the **AI provider** and **AI model** powering your agent (see [Voice Profile - LLM](/platform/studio-view/voice-profile#llm)). The list shows the providers and models available in your workspace, for example:

| Provider | Model | Best For |
|----------|-------|----------|
| OpenAI | gpt-4o | General purpose, high accuracy |
| Groq | llama-3.1-70b | Low latency, cost-efficient |
| Google | gemini-1.5-flash | Multimodal, long context |
| Azure OpenAI | gpt-4o | Enterprise compliance |

For support bots, `gpt-4o` or `llama-3.1-70b` via Groq are solid starting points. Click **Save**.

---

## Step 5: Add a Knowledge Base

Upload product documentation, FAQs, or policy docs so your agent can answer accurately.

1. In the left sidebar, open **Knowledge Base** and click **Add**.
2. Set **Content Type** to **Document** and click **Save**.
3. Open the new knowledge base and drag your files onto the upload panel, then click **Upload**. Supported formats include PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT and Markdown (see [Knowledge Base](/platform/studio-view/knowledge-base) for the full list).
4. In **AI Studio**, open your agent and go to **Persona → Knowledge**. Click **Add**, select the knowledge base in the **Select knowledge bases** drawer, click **Add (1)**, then click **Save** at the bottom right of the Persona tab.

Unpod uses RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) - the agent searches relevant chunks before generating a response.

**Tips for better retrieval:**
- Break large PDFs into topic-focused sections
- Include an FAQ document with exact question phrasing customers use
- Add a "what not to say" policy document to constrain agent behavior

---

## Step 6: Set Up Telephony

Your agent needs a phone number to receive calls.

<Note>
This step is the dashboard flow. If you attach a number to an agent from the SDK instead, the bridge is created and managed for you - see [Attach a Number to an Agent](/platform/dev-platform/attach-number-to-agent).
</Note>

In the left sidebar, click **Telephony**:

1. The **Telephony** panel lists the numbers assigned to your workspace. If it is empty, click **+** and pick a number in the **Available Numbers** drawer.
2. Select the number.
3. On the **Integration** tab, under **Number Configuration**, open **Select Agent** and pick your agent (Aria).
4. Click **Save**.

Incoming calls to this number now route to Aria. See [Telephony](/platform/studio-view/telephony) for details.

---

## Step 7: Test the Agent

1. Click **Talk To Agent** at the top right of the agent page to test it in the browser
2. Call the provisioned phone number from your mobile
3. Check **Call Logs** to review the transcript and see how the agent performed

---

## Monitoring & Iteration

After going live:

- **Call Logs**: Review transcripts to find gaps in knowledge base coverage
- **Analytics**: Track call duration, resolution rate, and sentiment over time
- **Analysis Tab**: Configure post-call extraction - pull structured data (intent, outcome, customer tier) from each conversation

Iterate on the system prompt and knowledge base based on real call patterns.
