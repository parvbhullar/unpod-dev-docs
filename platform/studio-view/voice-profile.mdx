## Voice Profile

A voice profile is a saved set of voice settings (LLM, transcriber and voice) that defines how your agent understands callers and how it sounds. You can reuse a saved profile or set every part by hand.

The **Voice Profile** tab is only shown for voice agents. Like the other agent tabs, it has a section list on the left; click a section to edit it on the right. The sections are **Voice Profile**, **LLM**, **Transcriber**, **Voice** and **Config**.

### Voice Profile

Reuse a saved voice profile. Click **Manage Profiles** (or **Select Voice Profile** if none is set yet) to open the **Manage Profiles** drawer and pick a profile. The LLM, Transcriber and Voice fields are filled in from the selected profile.

This section also shows the estimated **COST** per minute and the **LATENCY** of the current setup. Hover on the cost bar to see the breakup by Platform, TTS, LLM and STT. The cost is an estimate, not the actual price.

### LLM

The language model that understands the caller and writes the agent's replies:
- **AI provider** - the service that hosts the model.
- **AI model** - the model from that provider.
- **Max Tokens** - the maximum number of tokens in each reply. Default: 150.
- **Temperature** - controls AI randomness: lower values give more predictable replies, higher values more creative ones. Default: 0.7.

### Transcriber

Speech-to-text settings:
- **Transcription Provider** - the service that converts speech to text.
- **Transcription model** - the model that processes the transcription.
- **Language for transcription** - the language for speech recognition.
- **STT Vocabulary** - words or phrases the transcriber should recognize. Click **Add Vocabulary** and fill in **Word / Phrase** and **Recognition Hint**.

### Voice

Text-to-speech settings and tuning:
- **Voice Provider** - the provider that gives your agent its voice.
- **Voice model** - the model used to generate the audio.
- **Synthesized voice** - the voice used for text-to-speech. To use a voice that isn't in the list, check **Add Voice ID Manually** and enter its ID.
- **Voice Prompt** - instructions for how the voice should speak.
- **Voice Temperature** and **Voice Speed** - tune the voice's variation and speaking speed.
- **Background Sound** and **Background Volume** - ambient sound played during the call and how loud it is. A high volume can compete with speech and hurt transcription quality.
- **Thinking Volume** - how loud the ambient sound plays while the agent is thinking. Keep it below the background volume, or it can mask the agent's first words.
- **Enable Filler** - adds filler words (e.g. "um", "uh") for more natural-sounding conversations.
- **TTS Pronunciation** - how specific words should be spoken. Click **Add Pronunciation** and fill in **Word / Phrase** and **Pronunciation**.

### Config

Custom key-value pairs available to the agent. Click **Add field** and fill in **Config Key** and **Config Value**.
