### Getting Started

Unpod Agents are AI-powered assistants that handle calls, chats, and tasks. With an agent, you can decide how it interacts with users, what knowledge it accesses, and how it communicates over the phone.

To open the Studio View, click **Studio View** at the bottom of the left sidebar. In the Studio View the same button reads **Space View** and takes you back. The Studio View part of the left sidebar has these sections:

- **AI Studio** - your agents
- **Knowledge Base** - your knowledge bases. See [Knowledge Base](/platform/studio-view/knowledge-base).
- **Telephony** - your phone numbers. See [Telephony](/platform/studio-view/telephony).
- **Configuration** - the organization home page

If you have no space yet, there is no view switch and the sidebar shows only these sections.

Click **Configuration** to open the organization home page. It shows **Welcome** followed by your first name, and three cards: **Create Space**, **Create AI Identity** and **Setup Telephony**.

You already created a Voice Agent for your business while getting access to the [dashboard](https://unpod.ai). You can change that agent for advanced requirements, or create a new one. Click the **Create AI Identity** card, or click **AI Studio** in the left sidebar. Both open the AI Studio page.

The AI Studio page lists your agents under **Agents**, with a search box, and opens the first agent in the list. Click an agent to see its configuration. To create a new agent, click the **+** button next to **Agents**. It takes you to the same agent creation page you saw during sign-up.
