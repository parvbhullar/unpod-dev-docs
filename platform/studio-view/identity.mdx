## Identity

The **Identity** tab holds how the agent is presented and who can use it. The agent's name is shown at the top of the agent page; click the pencil icon next to it to rename the agent.

The sections in the left list are **Basics**, **Category**, **Contact** and **Social Links**.

### Basics

Description, avatar and privacy:
- **Description** (required) - a short description of what the agent does, its domain and its main purpose. It is used in agent listings and the publish modal.
- **Avatar** - click **Upload image** to add a PNG or JPG up to 5MB.
- **Card Background** - the background gradient shown on the agent's discover card. Pick one or choose **Custom**.
- **Who can use this agent** (required) - **Everyone (public agent marketplace)** or **Workspace members only**. With **Workspace members only**, add the people under **Shared with**.

### Category

Tags and purpose:
- **Category** - pick a category from the list.
- **Tags** (required) - keywords that help users discover your agent. Add at least one.
- **Purpose** (required) - **Business Functions**, **Personal Functions** or **Service Functions**.

### Contact

- **Contact Number** - pick a telephony number assigned to this workspace.
- **Address** - a physical or mailing address shown alongside the agent profile.

### Social Links

Public profiles and website. Click **Add social link**, then choose the **Platform** and enter the **URL**.

When you're done, click **Save** at the bottom right of the tab.

<Note>
When you create a new agent, only the Identity tab is enabled until the agent is saved.
</Note>
