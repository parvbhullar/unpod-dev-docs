## Call Logs

You can download the details of the calls made in a space as a CSV file.

1. Open the space in the Space View.
2. At the top right of the space page, click the three-dot menu (⋮) and click **Call Logs**.
3. The **Download Call Logs** drawer opens. To narrow the download, fill in any of these filters, or leave them empty to download everything:
   - **From** and **To** - a date and time range
   - **Run IDs** - one or more call runs
   - **Agents** - one or more agents
   - **User** - a member of the space
   - **Contact Numbers (comma-separated)**
   - **Document ID**
4. Click **Download**. The CSV file is saved to your computer.

The three-dot menu is shown only to users who can edit the space.
