## Integration

The **Integration** tab connects the agent to other systems. Each integration appears as a section in the list on the left: the integrations configured for your workspace (such as the webhook) and **Google Sheet Sync**. Click a section to edit it on the right.

### Webhook

Webhook integration is the process of using webhooks to enable real-time communication between web applications, where one application sends data to another as an event occurs.

{/* TODO(UNPO-403): new screenshot — webhook section */}

To enable the webhook integration, you have to click on Enable Webhook as Yes.

Webhook URL is the endpoint which will be provided by the user.

You can also add some headers while doing this integration if needed. Webhook headers are the key-value pairs for identification, authentication, and context, telling the receiver who sent it, how to process data, and event details.

#### Common Webhook Headers

**Common and Standard Headers:**
- **Content-Type** - Describes the format of the data (e.g., application/json, application/x-www-form-urlencoded).
- **User-Agent** - Identifies the client sending the request (e.g., GitLab/15.5.0).
- **Content-Length** - Size of the request body in bytes.

**Security and Authentication Headers:**
- **Authorization** - For bearer tokens or basic auth (e.g., Bearer token).
- **X-Hub-Signature / X-Hub-Signature-256** - HMAC signature to verify the request authenticity (GitHub, etc.).
- **X-Shopify-Hmac-Sha256** - The signature of Shopify to verify the delivery.
- **Idempotency-Key** - Ensures a request is processed only once, even with retries.

**Platform-Specific Headers (Examples):**
- **X-GitHub-Event** - Type of event (e.g., push, pull_request).
- **X-Shopify-Topic** - The event topic (e.g., products/create).
- **X-Gitlab-Event** - GitLab event type (e.g., Push Hook).
- **X-Contentful-Topic** - Event topic in Contentful.

### Google Sheet Sync

Append every finished call as a row to a Google Sheet you control.

The agent must be saved before you can connect a sheet.

1. Open **Google Sheet Sync** and turn the switch on.
2. Choose the sheet:
   - With your Google account connected with Drive access, pick one of your sheets from the **Google Sheet** list, or click **Create new** and enter a **Sheet title** (the sheet is created in your own Google Drive).
   - Otherwise, paste the **Google Sheet URL** (open the sheet in Google Sheets and copy its link from the address bar) and click **Connect**. Without your own Google account, the platform service account writes to the sheet, so share the sheet with it as Editor. **Connect your own Google account instead** switches to your account.
3. Click **Manage sheet** to set the **Sheet tab** (created on the first sync if missing; left blank, it defaults to "Call Logs") and **Pick columns** — columns are written left to right in the order you pick them. **Open in Google Sheets** opens the sheet.

These changes apply right away; they don't wait for the tab's **Save** button. From then on, every finished call is appended as a row. Turning the switch off pauses writing but keeps the sheet and its sync position. **Disconnect this sheet** (in **Manage sheet**) stops syncing new calls until you attach another sheet.
