## Numbers: Your Voice, Your Number, Any Region

In Unpod, numbers are virtual phone lines that let your business make and receive calls or messages from anywhere in the world, without using traditional phone lines. Each number can connect to a SIP trunk or an agent, which helps create flexible and smart communication.

### Add a Number

#### Step 1
Open **Telephony → Numbers** and click the **+** button next to the **Telephony** heading. Numbers you have already added are listed below it.

#### Step 2
**Available Numbers** lists every number you can add. Use the search box to find one, click **Select** on it, then click **Save**.

#### Step 3
If a number shows **Upgrade** instead of **Select**, your plan has no free channel left. Click **Upgrade** to open **Add Subscription Add-ons**, add more phone numbers or channels to your plan, then select the number.

#### Step 4
The number is added to your list and starts **Not linked**. Click **Connect to** and choose **SIP Trunk** or **Agents**. See [Connect an Unpod Number](/telephony/integrations/unpod/dashboard) for the SIP trunk setup.
