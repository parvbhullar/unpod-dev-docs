## SIP Trunking: Powering Seamless Global Connections

SIP trunking replaces traditional phone lines with a virtual connection over the internet, enabling your business to make and receive calls through a broadband connection. It links your internal phone system (PBX or VoIP) to a SIP provider, which then routes calls to the regular phone network. This setup makes your communication system simpler and usually more affordable.

## Supported SIP Providers: Bridging You to the World Beyond

Each of these providers connects to your Unpod number over a SIP trunk. Each guide covers the Unpod side and the provider side:

- **[Vapi](/telephony/integrations/vapi/dashboard)** - Provides cloud telephony API for initiating and receiving phone calls programmatically.
- **[LiveKit](/telephony/integrations/livekit/dashboard)** - Enables real time audio and video calls over the internet using virtual members.
- **[ElevenLabs](/telephony/integrations/elevenlabs/dashboard)** - Answers calls with an ElevenLabs conversational agent.
- **[Ultravox](/telephony/integrations/ultravox/dashboard)** - Places outbound AI voice calls through your Unpod number.
- **[Daily](/telephony/integrations/daily/dashboard)** - Powers multi-party voice and video communication with web-based integrations.

## Setup Process

Every provider uses the same steps on the Unpod side. Add a number first ([Numbers](/platform/dev-platform/numbers)).

#### Step 1
In **Telephony → Numbers**, select your number. It shows **Not linked**. Click **Connect to** and choose **SIP Trunk**. Unpod creates the number's outbound trunk.

#### Step 2
Under **Outbound Trunk Details**, copy the **Address**, **Port**, **Protocol**, **Username** and **Password**. The provider uses these to send calls through your Unpod number.

#### Step 3
Under **Inbound Trunk Details**, click **Connect**. On the **New trunk** tab, enter a **Name** and the provider's SIP host in **SIP URI / address**, set **Port** and **Transport**, then click **Create Trunk**. Unpod sends incoming calls on your number to that host.

#### Step 4
Finish the setup in the provider's console with the values from Step 2. The provider guides above walk through it.
