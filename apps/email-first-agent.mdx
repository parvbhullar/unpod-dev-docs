**Illustrative.** An agent that lives in the inbox: it answers inbound
email, follows up on its own schedule, and places a call when a
conversation needs a voice. Built by SaaS teams and support operations.

## How it would work

<Steps>
  <Step title="An email arrives">
    It resolves to a person and to their open thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent replies, or waits">
    It answers now, or schedules a follow-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No reply in two days">
    The flow places a call, with the thread as context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The outcome is written back">
    The thread, and the person, carry what happened on the call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Build it today

Nothing email-native ships yet. The closest working version keeps email in
your own stack and puts the voice step on Unpod:

1. Your system sends and receives the email.
2. When a thread needs a voice, your system places a call and passes the
   thread summary as call data.
3. Attach an [analytics block](/speech-stack/analytics) to the agent, read
   the outcome with `client.analytics.for_call(call.call_id)`, and write it
   back to the thread.

```python call_from_thread.py
import asyncio
from unpod import AsyncClient

async def main() -> None:
    async with AsyncClient() as client:
        call = await client.calls.create(
            agent_id="renewals",
            to_number="+9198xxxxxx01",
            data={"thread_summary": "Asked about the renewal price twice; no reply since Monday."},
        )
        print(call.call_id, call.status)

asyncio.run(main())
```
